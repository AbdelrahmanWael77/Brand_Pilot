# FlowNest — Approved Demo Facts / Copy Restrictions

**Demo only; fictional product and synthetic brand.** No customer records, real results or accounts.

## Authorized product facts

| Fact ID | Canonical statement |
|---|---|
| `FN-001` | FlowNest offers shared team task boards. |
| `FN-002` | FlowNest supports task assignments. |
| `FN-003` | FlowNest supports reminders. |
| `FN-004` | FlowNest provides a browser-based dashboard. |
| `FN-005` | FlowNest offers a 14-day free trial. |
| `FN-006` | The primary CTA in English is **Start your free trial.** |

**Audience:** small and medium-sized teams in Egypt.  
**Tone:** approachable, clear, confident.  
**Languages:** English and Arabic, with Egyptian-market localization.

## Authorized implications and boundaries

- May describe organization and teamwork qualitatively **without implying measured productivity improvement**; tie concrete statements to the four product features.
- May explain that shared boards, assignments and reminders are available together in a browser-based dashboard, if wording accurately represents supported facts.
- Arabic CTA may be a natural meaning-preserving equivalent of the approved English CTA. Final Arabic wording requires native human review.
- The `14-day free trial` is an authorized **product offer duration**, not an automatically editable marketing-only date. A change to 21 days is **not authorized** without new fact approval.

## Prohibited/unsupported without new human approval

- Customer count, market leadership/rank, discount or price, guaranteed time savings, productivity multiplier, testimonials, quotes attributed to customers, security certification, data residency, integrations, mobile apps, project-management AI, automated workflows, enterprise SSO, or promises about future results.
- Do not write "free forever," "no credit card required," "cancel anytime," "ISO certified," "trusted by thousands," or "best productivity platform" without authorization.
- Don't imply an endorsement by a real Egyptian organization.

## Product-approved vs campaign-controlled fields

- **Product-controlled:** fact IDs, features, 14-day trial authorization. Updating these requires explicit human approval and new authoritative fact/version record.
- **Campaign-controlled:** creative theme, channel phrasing, audience subset, visual layout, approved CTA/localized CTA, campaign run dates, brand palette and tone (subject to owner approval). Changing them must version briefs and invalidate dependent reviews.

## Test fixture rule

For offer-duration tests, use a **separate authorized test revision** (a synthetic fact registry with the new duration) rather than falsely changing FlowNest's approved 14-day promise.
